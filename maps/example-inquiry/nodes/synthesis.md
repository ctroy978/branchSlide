# Synthesis

## What We Learned

No single ethical framework resolves every dilemma. A complete moral education requires:

- **Virtue ethics** — cultivating wise character over time
- **Consequentialism** — honestly weighing outcomes
- **And the courage** to sit with uncertainty

The gardener reminds us that ethics is never purely abstract. Real people, real beauty, real consequences.

> *The goal of inquiry is not agreement — it is understanding.*
