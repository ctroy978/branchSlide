# The Dilemma

A runaway trolley is heading toward five people tied to the tracks. You stand next to a lever that would divert it onto a side track — where one person is working.

**But wait:** the one person is a gardener planting flowers that bring joy to an entire neighbourhood.

> What ought you to do — and *why*?

This is not a trick question. There is no single correct answer. Your reasoning matters more than your conclusion.
