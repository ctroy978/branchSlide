# Consequentialism

Utilitarians like Bentham and Mill ask a simpler question: *Which action produces the greatest good for the greatest number?*

By the numbers:

- **Do nothing** → 5 deaths
- **Pull the lever** → 1 death

The gardener's flowers benefit hundreds — but can we weigh aesthetic joy against a human life?

Mill distinguished **higher** and **lower** pleasures. Does that change your calculation?
