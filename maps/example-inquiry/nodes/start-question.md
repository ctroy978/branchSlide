## Which lens should we use first?

We have heard the dilemma. Before I choose our path, think carefully:

- **Virtue ethics** asks what a person of practical wisdom would *become* — not just what they would do in one moment.
- **Consequentialism** asks us to weigh outcomes honestly — but can every good be measured?

Which framework do you want to test against the gardener's story? Be ready to say why.
