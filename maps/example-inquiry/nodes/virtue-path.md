# Virtue Ethics

Aristotle would ask: *What would a person of practical wisdom (phronesis) do here?*

Virtue ethics focuses on **character**, not rules or outcomes:

- Courage — acting rightly despite fear
- Justice — giving each their due
- Compassion — recognising shared humanity

Consider the gardener. Is there something virtuous in protecting the one who creates beauty for many?

```text
Virtue  →  Character  →  Habit  →  Flourishing (eudaimonia)
```
